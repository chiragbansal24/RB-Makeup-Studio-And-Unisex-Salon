RB MAKEUP STUDIO WEBSITE - HOW TO HOST ON GITHUB PAGES
=======================================================
1. Unzip this folder. Keep index.html, sitemap.xml, robots.txt and the assets folder together.
2. On github.com create a new public repository (for example: rb-makeup).
3. Click "Add file" > "Upload files", drag in EVERYTHING from the unzipped folder, then Commit.
4. Go to Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: main > Folder: / (root) > Save.
5. After a minute GitHub shows your site address, like https://username.github.io/rb-makeup/

AFTER IT IS LIVE (send the address to Claude, or do it yourself):
- Replace  https://YOUR-SITE-URL/  with your real address (keep the ending slash) in:
  index.html (5 places), sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
- In index.html find  G-XXXXXXXXXX  and replace it with your Google Analytics Measurement ID.
- Submit your address and sitemap.xml in Google Search Console (search.google.com/search-console).
- Add the same address to your Google Business Profile and both Instagram pages.
