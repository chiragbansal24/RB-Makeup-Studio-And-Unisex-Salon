About Rupal Bansal, Bridal Makeup Artist in Ghaziabad

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about bridal makeup in Ghaziabad and Delhi NCR.

How much does bridal makeup cost in Ghaziabad?

Bridal makeup at RB Makeup Studio starts from ₹20,000. Engagement starts from ₹15,000, Haldi & Mehendi from ₹8,000 and Cocktail from ₹7,000. The final quote depends on your date, look and venue, and is confirmed on WhatsApp.

What is the price of HD, glass look and basic makeup?

HD makeup starts from ₹4,000, glass look makeup from ₹5,000 and basic makeup from ₹3,000.

Is Rupal Bansal a Preeti Gera certified makeup artist?

Yes. Rupal Bansal holds a Certificate of Completion from Preeti Gera for the Basic to Advance Makeup & Hair Course, and has 3+ years of experience including freelance bridal work.

Do you travel to the wedding venue in Delhi NCR?

Yes, Rupal offers on-location makeup across Ghaziabad and Delhi NCR. Travel charges depend on the venue location.

Which makeup brands do you use?

Only top-tier luxury international brands, including Dior, NARS, MAC, Laura Mercier, Huda Beauty, Rare Beauty and Shopaarel.

What is the difference between HD makeup and glass look makeup?

HD makeup is built for a flawless, camera-ready finish that stays sharp in photos and videos. Glass look makeup gives a dewy, glowing, skin-first finish. Rupal can recommend the right one for your skin and outfit.

How do I book my date?

Tap any Book button or message us on WhatsApp at +91 88827 13130 with your event date, venue and the look you want. We will confirm availability. Message early once your date is fixed, because wedding dates fill quickly.

Where is the studio located?

RB Makeup Studio and Unisex Salon is at C-23, Ramprastha, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh.
